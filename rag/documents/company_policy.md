# Company Expense and Travel Policy

## Section 1.1: Daily Meal Allowance
Employees traveling on company business are entitled to a fixed daily meal allowance of $75 per day without requiring individual itemized receipts.

## Section 1.2: Advance Flight Bookings
All domestic airfare must be booked at least 14 days in advance in standard economy class. Any last-minute exceptions require Vice President approval.

## Section 1.3: Accommodation Caps
Hotel room rates are reimbursed up to $200 per night for standard tier cities, and up to $300 per night for designated metropolitan cities (New York, San Francisco, London).
