SenseAI Hardware Support and Warranty Terms

Standard Warranty Duration:
The SenseAI smart voice unit includes a 2-year limited manufacturer hardware warranty beginning from the verified date of delivery.

Component Replacement:
Defective microphone array boards and acoustic transducers will be repaired or replaced free of charge within 48 hours of service claim submission.

Exclusions:
Damage caused by liquid immersion, unauthorized disassembly, or electrical surges is explicitly excluded from standard warranty coverage and requires the SenseCare Protection tier.
